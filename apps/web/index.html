    Getting started

    1. Pick an example or write your own firmware in main.c.
    2. Press ▶ Run. GCC compiles main.c; Renode runs the ELF on a simulated TM4C123.
    3. Interact: hold SW1/SW2 or a button, turn a potentiometer, watch LEDs and the LCD.
    4. Build circuits: Add Part, then drag from one pin to another to wire them.
    5. Errors open in Problems; click one to jump to its line (F8 for the next).
